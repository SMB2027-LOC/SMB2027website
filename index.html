  • An excursion on Wednesday afternoon, followed by the banquet at the University of San Diego
  • Registration and pre-meeting workshops on Sunday, June 27

Hosts

SMB 2027 is hosted by the Computational Science Research Center at San Diego State University, with the University of San Diego. We look forward to welcoming the mathematical biology community to San Diego for a week of science, collaboration, and collegial exchange.
