Minisymposium guidelines

  • Each session is 100 minutes and has four 25-minute talks on a common scientific theme.
  • A minisymposium may request one, two, three, or four sessions.
  • Each minisymposium has one lead organizer and up to two co-organizers.
  • Organizers build the speaker list and confirm that every speaker will take part before submitting.
  • A participant may give at most one minisymposium talk. Education talks are an exception. Contributed talks are allowed in addition.

What the proposal asks for

  • Lead organizer name, affiliation, and email
  • Co-organizer names and affiliations
  • Title, up to 100 characters
  • Description, up to 1500 words
  • Relevant SMB subgroup
  • Number of sessions requested
  • Confirmed speakers with affiliation and tentative title
